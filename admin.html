Common questions

Are your products authentic?

Yes. We source sealed stock from authorized distributors. Batch numbers and expiry dates are visible on the packaging when your order arrives. If anything looks off, refuse the delivery and contact us.

What is the difference between Zonnic and Velo?

Zonnic is positioned as a nicotine replacement therapy (NRT) pouch for adults reducing or quitting smoking, with a slower, steadier release. Velo is a recreational nicotine pouch with a faster onset. Both are tobacco-free.

How do I pay?

M-Pesa Till 8435626, or cash on delivery. After you send your cart summary on WhatsApp, we confirm stock and send the Till prompt details.

What if I receive a wrong or defective item?

Contact us on WhatsApp within 48 hours with a photo of the item. Sealed, unused wrong items are replaced. Defective devices are replaced or refunded after we confirm the fault.

Do you check age on delivery?

Yes. Riders check ID on delivery. We do not hand orders to anyone under 18. First-time WhatsApp orders may require an ID confirmation before dispatch.
